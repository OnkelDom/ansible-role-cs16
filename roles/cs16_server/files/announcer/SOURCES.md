# Classic announcer sound provenance

These WAV files use the classic filenames documented by the AMX Mod X
"Ultimate Sounds" community plugin:

- AlliedModders plugin thread: https://forums.alliedmods.net/showthread.php?t=66006
- AlliedModders setup reference: https://forums.alliedmods.net/showthread.php?t=26821

The copies in this role were retrieved from the following public Counter-Strike
FastDL mirror on 2026-07-18:

https://fastdl.gamecs.com.br/cstrike/sound/misc/

`knifekill.wav` is the classic `humiliation.wav`, and `grenadekill.wav` is the
classic `perfect.wav`, renamed to match the LenMail plugin event names.

The audio remains subject to the rights of its original creators. Review your
redistribution requirements before publishing or deploying this role.
